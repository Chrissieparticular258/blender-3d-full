This version includes the standard toolset for 3D design:

*   Modeling Tools: Use mesh, curve, and sculpting tools to build detailed geometry.
*   Animation System: Build keyframe sets and use onion skinning for smooth movement.
*   Rendering Engine: Use the built-in path tracer for realistic lighting and shadows.
*   AI Integration: Access generative tools to speed up your texturing and asset creation.
*   Sculpting: Adjust high-polygon models with brush-based tools.
*   Multi-Format Export: Save your work in common formats like OBJ, FBX, and GLTF.

## 🖥 Using the interface

When you open the application, you see a 3D viewport. The large area in the center shows your current project. The panels on the sides house your tools and properties.

*   Selection: Click the left mouse button to select an object.
*   Movement: Hold the middle mouse button to rotate your view around the center.
*   Tool Shelf: Look at the left side of your screen to change how you manipulate objects.
*   Properties: Look at the right side of your screen to change settings like color, size, and material.
*   Menus: Use the top bar to save, load, or export your work.

## 💡 Common tasks

Users often perform these tasks first:

*   Creating a Shape: Use the Add menu at the top of the viewport to place a cube or sphere into your scene. 
*   Changing Views: Use your mouse scroll wheel to zoom in and out of your 3D space.
*   Applying Materials: Click the checkerboard icon on the right panel to assign colors or textures to your object.
*   Lighting: Add a light source from the Add menu to illuminate your model for the final render.

Keep your projects organized. Create a main folder for your 3D work. Inside that, create a separate folder for every individual project you start. This ensures that when you save textures or linked files, the software can find them later. Always export your final render as an image file so you can view it outside of the application.
